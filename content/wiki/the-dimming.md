**The Dimming** was the night on which every known [[pylon]] stopped broadcasting. It is the reference point of the modern calendar: years before it are counted *BD* ("before the Dimming") and years after it *PD* ("past the Dimming").

## The night

Accounts agree that nothing visible happened. The lamps were not seen to fail, because they were not usually lit. It was only in the weeks afterwards, as the guild's watchers compared ledgers, that anyone noticed there had been no broadcast anywhere since the turn of the year.[^first]

## Aftermath

For the first generation after the Dimming, life in [[Halden]] went on largely as before; existing [[insignia]] were still bright and still readable. The consequences arrived slowly. Children were born without insignia. Property had to be recorded in ink. The [[Lantern Guild]] turned from keepers of broadcasts into keepers of copies.

> The towers did not go dark. We did.
> — attributed to [[Maren Oake]]

## Theories

- **Exhaustion** — the pylons had a finite number of broadcasts, and used them.
- **Completion** — every name that needed to be given had been given.
- **Withdrawal** — the pylons stopped *choosing* to broadcast.

None of these has any evidence beyond the fact of the silence itself.

[^first]: Watchers' circular of 0 PD, reprinted in the guild's *Book of Lamps*.
