**Halden** is a harbour city on a crescent-shaped bay, built around a hill that carries seven [[pylon]]s. Before [[the Dimming]] it was the most "lit" city of its region: more [[insignia]] were broadcast from its lamps than from any other group of pylons on record.

## Name

The city is named for the seventh lamp on its hill, which stands a little apart from the other six. *Hal* is an old word for a lamp set apart; *den* is a hill.

## Layout

The old town climbs the hill in rings, each ring older than the one below it. The six inner pylons stand in the uppermost ring, around the guild hall of the [[Lantern Guild]]. The seventh stands on a spur above the harbour, reachable only by a stair cut into the rock.

## Life after the Dimming

Halden has become a city of copies. Painted insignia cover lintels and shutters; the guild's copy-rooms are the busiest rooms in the city; and a trade in *witnessed* copies — ones a guild reader has certified against a living bearer — supports many families.
