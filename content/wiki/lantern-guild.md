The **Lantern Guild** is the guild of readers and watchers that has kept the [[pylon]]s of [[Halden]] for as long as there have been records. Before [[the Dimming]] its members watched the lamps, recorded every broadcast in the guild's ledgers and read [[insignia]] for anyone who could pay. Since the Dimming the guild's work has turned almost entirely to copying.

## Organization

The guild is led by a Warden, currently [[Maren Oake]]. Below the Warden are *readers*, who are trained to read insignia at a glance, and *watchers*, who keep the night watch on the lamps. Apprentices serve both for seven years before choosing.

## The yearly climb

Once a year, on the anniversary of the Dimming, watchers climb every dark pylon in Halden and sit at its crown until dawn, "in case one answers". The custom is widely mocked and never skipped.
